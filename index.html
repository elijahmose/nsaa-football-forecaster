Ratings are in points above the average team in the format. The likely score is the single most probable realistic final: the expected margin and a typical total for the format, nudged to scores that actually occur (multiples of touchdowns, extra points and field goals). It can sit a few points off the expected margin, and any one exact score is rarely more than a 1 to 2 percent chance. The margin range covers the middle half of games with a prediction like this one (a quarter land on each side outside it); the 80% range is wider, about plus or minus 22 points in 11-man, 26 in 8-man and 31 in 6-man. Single games vary a lot, and a rating gap tells you more about who wins than about the exact score. Teams can only meet inside a format, because 11-man, 8-man and 6-man ratings are not comparable.

Set the seeds

MRI score = 50 + 15 x (power weight x power z + resume weight x resume z). Power is NFPI Projected Power (offense plus defense, with last season as the starting point). Resume is wins minus the wins a top-20% team would be expected to have against the same schedule, per 9 games. Margins do not count in resume; forfeit results do. At 70/30 the blend cost nothing in forecast accuracy in a week-by-week test; lower power weights start to lose accuracy.
